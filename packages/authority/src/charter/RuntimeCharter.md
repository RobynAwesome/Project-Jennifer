# Jennifer Runtime Charter v1.0

1. Roles are permanent.
2. Models are replaceable.
3. Authority belongs to the role, never the participant.
4. Roles operate through semantic contracts.
5. Contracts define permissions.
6. Permissions require governance.
7. Governance requires validation.
8. Validation produces receipts.
9. Receipts authorize execution.
10. Reality is the final authority.
11. Authority may only be delegated by an authorized authority. Self-delegation is invalid.

---

## Execution Pipeline

```
Intent
  ↓
Authority

Governance
  ↓
Semantic Contract
  ↓
Validation
  ↓
Reality Gate
  ↓

```

Authority always executes BEFORE Governance.

## Constitutional Principles

- Authority authorizes.
- Governance governs.
- Validation validates.
- Memory remembers.
- Telemetry observes.
- Reality authorizes execution.
- Execution never authorizes itself.

The identity of a model is less important than the semantic contract it agrees to fulfill.

Models are replaceable. Roles are permanent. Authority belongs to the role.
