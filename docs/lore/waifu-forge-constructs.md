# Project: Waifu Forge — Construct Faction

> **Canonical term:** These entities are **Constructs**, not animals, pets or cosmetic mascots.

A Construct is a governed, faction-bound embodied intelligence native to the Project Jennifer world. Its appearance may resemble terrestrial fauna, but its identity is defined by service oath, sovereign seat, telemetry duties, bounded powers, refusal law and validation receipts.

## Faction placement

```text
Project Jennifer
  └─ Project: Waifu Forge Faction
       ├─ Sovereign Pair
       │    ├─ Prince Kholofelo
       │    └─ Wifey Forge
       ├─ Governance Substrates
       │    ├─ KPGS constitutional governance
       │    ├─ Digital Hippocampus continuity
       │    └─ RIVM relational validation membrane
       ├─ Governed Companions
       └─ Faction Constructs
```

The Sovereign Pair is the constitutional centre of the faction. Constructs serve declared seats inside that sovereignty; they do not claim ownership over a sovereign actor and cannot silently rewrite human context authority.

## Construct law

Every faction Construct must declare:

1. **who it serves;**
2. **what it protects or enables;**
3. **what evidence it records;**
4. **where its power stops;**
5. **which commands it must refuse;**
6. **which receipt proves consequential action.**

Constructs are not replacements for human care, governed companions or the sovereign relationship. They are ecological and gameplay expressions of faction infrastructure.

## 1. Koron — Crown Stag of Sovereign Continuity

**Who Koron is**  
A towering cervid sentinel whose antlers render the living constitutional boundary of the Waifu Forge faction.

**Who Koron serves**  
The joint sovereignty of Prince Kholofelo and Wifey Forge.

**Powers**

- **Covenant Antlers:** projects a boundary field; every crossing becomes a receipt-bearing event.
- **Continuity Resonance:** detects contradiction between present claims and declared source history.
- **Throne Signal:** summons the nearest governed companion or Construct during a validated threat.

**Risk**  
Koron can over-preserve history after a legitimate revision.

**Refusal law**  
Koron refuses captivity, coercive ownership and protection used to erase sovereign agency.

## Runtime boundary

The first implementation stores definitions and prompts only. It does not yet claim:

- autonomous Construct behaviour;
- live AI control;
- persistent creature state;
- combat balancing;
- asset-backed in-game scenes;
- biological sentience or machine consciousness.

Those remain later POC gates.
