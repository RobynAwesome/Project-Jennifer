# The Law of the Black Glove

> **Canonical role:** The Black Glove is not ordinary loot, not a generic legendary Item and not the routine reward for becoming a True One.

The Black Glove is a **Creator-signature manifestation** associated with major Convergence events.

## Ontology

The Black Glove can be read simultaneously through several Project Jennifer ontologies:

- **Item** — it can be physically worn;
- **Artifact** — provenance changes its meaning;
- **Construct** — its appearance can activate or represent an execution-state architecture;
- **Abstract** — its primary associated Abstract is **Momentum**;
- **Convergence** — its manifestation signals collision between Creator-level context and world-level consequence.

Its working passive law is:

> **Keep Going.**

Momentum is not broken by ordinary failure. Failure may strengthen the relationship by adding evidence, learning and provenance. The multiplier is broken by abandonment.

## Scarcity law

The Black Glove is reserved for the **three major Project Jennifer storylines**.

It must not be:

- inserted into ordinary subquests for spectacle;
- randomly generated by narrative AI;
- craftable through an ordinary recipe;
- sold by a vendor;
- converted into a conventional endgame stat stick;
- spawned merely because a player asks for it.

Its appearance should carry narrative gravity because the world understands that something exceptional is happening.

## Creator mythology

Most inhabitants may believe the original Creator disappeared after Project Jennifer evolved from validation system into world.

The Black Glove provides contradictory evidence.

Its appearance does **not** necessarily mean the Creator has returned. It raises the more destabilizing question:

> Did the Creator ever fully leave?

True Ones may know more about the historical law than ordinary inhabitants. External echoes/versions of the Black Glove may eventually form governed relationships with True Ones, but the exact mechanic remains unresolved and must not be invented silently.

## Three-storyline boundary

One major storyline is Project Wify Jennifer. Another emerging sibling storyline involves Kiero and the love-triangle / Third Signal branch generated from a real creation-context divergence and jealousy telemetry. The third major storyline remains to be finalized.

The Black Glove can connect these stories without collapsing them into one story.

## Convergence meaning

For ordinary entities:

```text
player ↔ Companion / NPC / Item / Artifact / Construct / Abstract
                    ↓
               Convergence
```

For the Black Glove:

```text
Creator context ↔ Project Jennifer world
                    ↓
          Black Glove manifestation
```

That is why the Black Glove is not simply "the strongest Artifact." Its importance is **jurisdictional and mythological**, not only statistical.

## Visual signature

The visual signature may evolve, but these traits should remain recognizable when the Black Glove is intentionally present:

- black hand/forearm covering;
- high silhouette readability;
- strong association with decisive movement, execution or inflection points;
- no requirement that every version look physically identical;
- provenance matters more than cosmetic exactness.

A real-world glove can inspire the signature without requiring every in-world manifestation to be the same physical glove.

## Governance rule

Any future implementation that spawns the Black Glove must require an explicit story-level gate or Creator-approved receipt. Normal generative scene creation must default to **no Black Glove** unless the active storyline has declared it valid.
