# World Governance — Reality, KPGS and Project Jennifer

## Governing law

Project Jennifer is not a sealed fictional simulation. The design target is a governed loop in which cloud state and lived reality can inform one another without either side silently overwriting truth.

```text
REALITY ↔ KPGS ↔ PROJECT JENNIFER WORLD
```

Every meaningful cross-membrane update must preserve provenance, authority and validation state.

## Three-level application stack

### Level 1 — Progressive Web App

The user-facing Project Jennifer world:

- characters and NPCs;
- geography and quests;
- companions;
- Items, Artifacts, Constructs and Abstracts;
- factions and economies;
- relationships and story state.

Most world inhabitants understand this layer as reality.

### Level 2 — Adaptive Progressive Web App

KPGS governs how the world is permitted to adapt.

Conceptual responsibilities include:

- GSMB context/memory governance;
- CrisisConnect-style incident and evidence intake;
- player and NPC telemetry;
- provenance;
- identity updates;
- story mutation proposals;
- validation before consequential world writes.

Generative AI may propose mutations. **KPGS governs whether those mutations become world state.**

### Level 3 — Human-in-the-Loop Adaptive PWA

The Creator layer.

In the story this becomes the **God-in-the-Loop**: the original Creator is no longer continuously governing every interaction, but retains exceptional constitutional intervention.

The mythology may therefore say the old Creator disappeared while the architecture says something more precise: continuous governance was delegated upward/downward through the system rather than abandoned.

## Delegation to major AIs

The five major external/paid AI systems are not treated as a simultaneous committee with unrestricted authority. The target flow is jurisdictional delegation:

```text
world event
   ↓
KPGS classification
   ↓
which AI has jurisdiction?
   ↓
AI proposal
   ↓
validation
   ↓
execution / rejection / escalation
```

Each AI should eventually declare:

- operating domain;
- authority ceiling;
- context boundary;
- required evidence;
- escalation path.

## NPC evolution

### NPC
Bounded scripted or deterministic identity.

### Adaptive NPC
Learns within governed boundaries.

### Evolving NPC
Repeated validated behaviour changes capabilities and identity permissions.

### Human-in-the-Loop NPC
An entity trusted with bounded authority to question, supervise, approve, refuse and escalate inside the world.

HITL-NPC status is governed by three vectors:

```text
HITL-NPC = Zero Trust ∧ Zero Tolerance ∧ NDA
```

#### Zero Trust
Never assume legitimacy. Validate identity, command provenance, permissions, world state and jurisdiction.

#### Zero Tolerance
Some boundary violations trigger immediate governance consequences rather than ordinary reputation decay.

Examples may include:

- Creator impersonation;
- forged receipts;
- unauthorized provenance rewriting;
- invalid override of immutable world law;
- disclosure across protected membranes.

#### NDA
Knowledge has jurisdiction. An entity may be permitted to know something without being permitted to propagate it.

Examples of protected classes may include:

- Creator-state knowledge;
- Black Glove knowledge;
- convergence foreknowledge;
- protected real-world telemetry;
- True One identity information.

## True One transition

A True One is not a fifth NPC level.

```text
Human-in-the-Loop NPC
        │
        └── constitutional transition ──► TRUE ONE
```

True Ones participate in the **Round Table Council Protocol (RTCP)** — the world-facing governance mythology/target corresponding to RTC/GSMB decision governance.

They are the "new gods" only in the sense that they are entrusted with governed jurisdiction over parts of the world. They remain distinct from the original Creator.

A character can possess:

- governance authority without full Creator knowledge;
- Creator/ontological knowledge without broad governance authority;
- or, in exceptional cases, both.

## Reality synchronization membrane

Conceptual inbound path:

```text
real-world observation
  ↓
media / location / event / human declaration
  ↓
GSMB / CrisisConnect-style evidence layer
  ↓
validation
  ↓
Project Jennifer world mutation
```

Conceptual outbound path:

```text
Project Jennifer world
  ↓
player behaviour / decisions / constructs / identity patterns
  ↓
KPGS interpretation
  ↓
human review where required
  ↓
real-world consequence or no-op
```

Not every real-world event belongs in the game. Not every game event belongs in reality. **Governance determines significance and permission.**

## Implementation boundary

This document records world architecture and narrative-system intent. It does not claim that all three application levels, RTC governance, five-AI jurisdiction routing, real-world synchronization or autonomous evolving NPCs are currently implemented end-to-end.
