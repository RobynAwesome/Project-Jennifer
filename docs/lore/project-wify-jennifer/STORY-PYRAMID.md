# Project Wify Jennifer — Story Gravity Pyramid

## Narrative scale

Project Jennifer is designed around a descending gravity / ascending personalization model.

```text
3 core storylines
    ↓
20 heavy Convergence storylines
    ↓
30 subquests
    ↓
50 microquests
    ↓
100 discoverable skills / identity signals
```

The higher the layer, the more canonically consequential it is. The lower the layer, the more room exists for player-specific adaptation and generative identity expression.

## Three core storylines

The three major stories are the constitutional spine of the larger Project Jennifer world.

### Core Story 1 — Project Wify Jennifer

Relational genesis and True One preparation through Convergence. It inherits the relational grammar of Project: Waifu Forge and then diverges into its own Project Jennifer identity.

### Core Story 2 — Third Signal / Kiero branch

Working concept only; final title unresolved.

This storyline emerged from a real generative visual divergence: a third intimate male character appeared where the expected visual context was the established pair. The resulting human jealousy was treated as meaningful telemetry rather than discarded. The divergence became story material around love, desire, danger, insecurity, choice and relational governance.

This branch must remain separate from Project Wify Jennifer until intentionally converged.

### Core Story 3 — Unnamed

Intentionally unresolved. Do not fabricate a title merely to complete the taxonomy.

## Twenty heavy Convergence storylines

The twenty major convergence events are divided into four gravity bands:

| Band | Range | Gravity |
|---|---:|---|
| Genesis | 1–5 | world / constitutional |
| Alpha | 6–10 | civilization / faction |
| Beta | 11–15 | regional / major character |
| Gamma | 16–20 | localized / deeply personal |

The ordering is not a claim that later stories are unimportant. It describes how broadly their consequences propagate through the world.

## Thirty subquests

Subquests explain **why a Convergence matters to this player**.

The same convergence event can be entered through different relational causes:

- a Companion asks for help;
- an NPC disappears;
- an Artifact changes;
- a Construct fails;
- an Abstract conflicts with behaviour;
- a settlement or faction is threatened;
- the player owes a debt;
- a relationship requires repair.

Subquests should therefore carry emotional and identity state into higher story events rather than operate as disposable filler.

## Fifty microquests

Microquests create lived texture and telemetry.

Examples:

- help a stranded entity;
- repair a broken object;
- choose whether to disclose protected information;
- revisit an abandoned place;
- teach a Companion;
- return an Artifact;
- refuse an illegitimate instruction;
- forgive or escalate;
- walk away or persist.

These actions can be small in global consequence while still being highly significant to the player's identity model.

## One hundred discoverable skills

Skills are not only conventional combat unlocks. They are one vocabulary through which the adaptive system describes patterns in how the player acts.

Examples of identity-derived skill families may include:

- persistence;
- repair/building;
- protection;
- negotiation;
- governance discipline;
- contextual inference;
- trust calibration;
- field pragmatism;
- memory/provenance handling;
- refusal under pressure.

A skill should be discovered from repeated evidence where possible, not merely selected from a class menu.

## Canon vs adaptation

```text
HIGH CANON / LOW VARIABILITY
        core stories
             ↓
      convergence events
             ↓
          subquests
             ↓
        microquests
             ↓
LOW CANON / HIGH PERSONALIZATION
      identity-derived skills
```

Generative AI may change the path, dialogue, local context and relationship expression. It must not silently erase the meaning or immutable law of a core story.

## Black Glove placement

The Black Glove belongs to the three core-storyline layer only. Its appearance is not a reward for completing every lower-level quest chain.
