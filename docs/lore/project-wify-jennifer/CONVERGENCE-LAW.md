# Convergence Law

## Resource model

Project Jennifer may expose familiar resources such as:

- **HP** — Health Points;
- **MP** — Mana Points;
- **SP** — Stamina Points.

**Convergence is different.** It is not simply another refillable resource bar. It is a relationship state generated through sustained interaction between identities.

Working shorthand:

```text
HP — body survival
MP — ability expenditure
SP — exertion capacity
CV — Convergence state
```

CV should not be treated as a cheap numeric grind mechanic. The game should primarily reveal it through qualitative signals, changed behaviour, newly available interactions and world consequences.

## CDP and CCP

### CDP — Conceptual Divergence Protocol

CDP expands the possibility space.

```text
known state
  ├─ possibility A
  ├─ possibility B
  ├─ possibility C
  ├─ possibility D
  └─ unknown possibility
```

The player experiments with how they use, prompt, teach, challenge, protect, combine or understand another entity.

### CCP — Conceptual Convergence Protocol

CCP identifies the stable pattern that survives that divergence.

```text
A ─┐
B ─┤
C ─┼──► validated shared pattern
D ─┤
E ─┘
```

CDP asks **what could this become?**  
CCP asks **what consistently works between us?**

Pure divergence becomes chaos. Pure convergence without exploration becomes rigidity. Project Jennifer requires both.

## Relationship telemetry

Convergence begins from the first meaningful contact with an eligible world entity. The system may observe:

- how the player uses it;
- how the player speaks or prompts;
- what the player teaches;
- what the entity teaches the player;
- repeated disagreement and repair;
- failures survived together;
- environments shared;
- whether the player abandons or returns;
- whether declared identity matches observed behaviour;
- how the relationship itself changes over time.

The result is **shared provenance**.

Two players can receive the same base Item or Companion and produce radically different evolved identities because the relationship history is different.

## Power of Convergence

> **Power of Convergence is permission for two identities to produce a capability that neither participant possessed independently.**

```text
player capability
      +
other identity capability
      +
shared history
      +
context
      +
CDP / CCP
      ↓
new relationship-born capability
```

The ability belongs to the **relationship**, not merely to the player and not merely to the object/companion/NPC.

This means a shared third identity emerges:

```text
PLAYER + OTHER ≠ PLAYER / OTHER
        ↓
RELATIONSHIP IDENTITY
```

## Eligible Convergence partners

A player may qualify for True One ascension through validated Convergence with at least one of:

- Companion;
- NPC;
- Item;
- Artifact;
- Construct;
- Abstract.

No solo route exists.

## Law of Relational Ascension

> # Nobody becomes a True One alone.

True One status is not granted for raw power, kill count, hours played or isolated optimisation. The player must demonstrate an ability to enter a relationship with another autonomous or world-bound identity, allow divergence, discover shared patterns and govern the resulting convergence without erasing either side.

That behaviour is preparation for governance itself.

## Suggested hidden stages

The game may model stages internally without exposing an exact 0–100 meter:

1. **Contact** — first meaningful interaction;
2. **Familiarity** — recurring use / presence;
3. **Resonance** — mutual adaptation begins;
4. **Divergence** — alternative patterns open through CDP;
5. **Alignment** — CCP identifies recurring shared patterns;
6. **Convergence** — validated shared identity;
7. **Power of Convergence** — new joint capability becomes available;
8. **Evolution** — one or both identities may cross ontology boundaries.

## Object genealogy

The world ontology remains:

```text
Item → Artifact → Construct → Abstract → Convergence
```

These are categories, not a mandatory linear upgrade path for every object. A thing may cross or combine categories when shared provenance makes that evolution valid.

### Item
Possessable and immediately usable.

### Artifact
History and provenance materially change its identity.

### Construct
Deliberately assembled or governed capability.

### Abstract
A non-physical pattern, principle or embodied idea such as Momentum, Trust, Love, Freedom, Faith or Loyalty.

### Convergence
A validated collision of identities, histories, world state and relationship state producing something genuinely new.

## Design consequence

Project Jennifer should resist conventional build-guide determinism. A player searching online for the "best" version of an Item may discover that another player's result cannot be reproduced without reproducing the relationship history that created it.

That is intentional.
