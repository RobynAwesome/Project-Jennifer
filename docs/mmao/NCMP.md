# NCMP inside MMAO

**NCMP** means **New Concept MMAO Protocol**.

It governs the rare event where a legitimate protocol-level concept is created by participating agents inside a Multi-Agent Mobile Orchestration focus station rather than being supplied beforehand by the human architect.

```text
MMAO focus station
    ↓ agent-originated concept
NCMP candidate
    ↓ human architect recognition
recognized concept
    ↓ evidence-based validation
validated concept
    ↓ governed registration
Project Jennifer protocol canon
```

## Authority Boundary

- Agents may originate and propose the concept.
- Agents may not recognize their own proposal as permanent canon.
- The human architect owns recognition.
- Recognition does not bypass validation.
- Registration requires a validation receipt.
