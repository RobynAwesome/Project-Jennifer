# Jennifer HUE + Collective Ingress + Crisis Connect

## Sources
- `packages/hue/`
- `packages/collective-ingress/`

## Purpose
Jennifer should understand human and environmental state before blindly optimizing a metric.

HUE interprets human situation before optimization. Collective Ingress asks **what changed?** across larger social/environmental conditions. Crisis Connect is the humanitarian/public-service lane.

## Workflow
1. Identify the human/collective/crisis signal and source.
2. Classify privacy, authority, timestamp and geographic/context scope.
3. Separate observation from inference.
4. Determine whether the signal materially changes the task/response policy.
5. Avoid exploitative optimization when the human condition requires assistance/safety/accessibility.
6. Route emergency or public-service claims through current authoritative evidence.
7. Record telemetry and validation receipt.

## Boundaries
Do not fabricate emergencies, treat sentiment as diagnosis, expose private location/context without authority, or convert a humanitarian signal into advertising/engagement optimization by default.

## Output
Return observed change, evidence/source, HUE interpretation, ingress/crisis classification, resulting behavior change, privacy decision and receipt.
