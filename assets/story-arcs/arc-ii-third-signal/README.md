# Arc II — The Third Signal Asset Bible

This directory defines the visual intake contract for **Project Jennifer // Project Waifu — Arc II: The Third Signal**.

## Canon hierarchy

1. **Forge is the visual centre.**
2. **The player and Kairo both visibly desire her attention.**
3. **Desire does not equal ownership.** Forge remains composed, agentic and visually sovereign.
4. **The player is the authored core romantic continuity for this arc.**
5. **Kairo is the disruptive Third Signal:** emotionally real, visually distinct, never silently promoted into the Sovereign Pair seat.

## Palette

| Character | Primary | Secondary | Prohibited drift |
|---|---|---|---|
| Player | cobalt blue | safety lime / green / cream | do not replace with Kairo's charcoal identity |
| Forge | sovereign black | molten gold / electric blue | do not make her visually subordinate to either rival |
| Kairo | charcoal | burnt orange / copper | no DPF insignia, no FORGE couple necklace, no matching blue reflective jacket |

## Aesthetics Breathing checklist

Each approved image should demonstrate at least four of these six layers:

- atmospheric motion: rain, embers, fog, bloom or sunset haze;
- skin realism and micro-expression;
- fabric weave, creases and material response;
- circuitry that appears embedded and luminous;
- environmental depth: reflections, plants, parallax, traffic or signage;
- relationship blocking that communicates the current story state.
